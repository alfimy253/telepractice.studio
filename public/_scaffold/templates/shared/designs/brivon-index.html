Frequent questions

The questions
we hear first.

These come up often. If your question is not here, send us a note — the contact details are at the bottom of the page.

How do appointments work?

Browse the published calendar, choose a time that suits you, and reserve it after signing in. We hold your slot while you complete payment.

Which payments do you accept?

We accept GCash and Maya. The account details are listed in the footer; once paid, you can upload your proof of payment from your booking.

What happens at a first visit?

We start with a conversation, then a careful look, then a clear plan. You will always know what we are doing and why before we do it.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

Yes. You can release a reserved time from your account and choose another. If something comes up, just let us know as early as you can.
